# US States — UX & data-viz review

**Date:** 2026-09-25 · **Build:** live site (`flourish-atlas.pages.dev/states`, `main`)
**Method:** used the page as a first-time visitor in Chrome:
- tried every control (both views, all three waves, Mean/Median, adjusted weights, a yes/no measure, a midyear measure);
- hovered states and dots;
- tested 1470, 1280 and 390 px, and resized between them;
- checked light and dark themes and the console.

I checked the adjusted-weight columns directly in `data/parquet/respondents.parquet`. Code was read only to confirm causes.

## Verdict

| Criterion | Score | One line |
|---|---|---|
| Visually coherent and appealing | 4 / 5 | Handsome map, calm palette, Alaska/Hawaii insets, pooled states outlined. |
| Easy to navigate | 3.5 / 5 | Map/Chart is already a one-chart-at-a-time switch. On phones, Chart, search and Topic hide in "More options". |
| Intuitive for a first-time user | 3 / 5 | A toggle that does nothing, two different "US overall" numbers, and "Hover a state" on a phone. |
| Understandable without technical knowledge | 2.5 / 5 | "Weights calibrated to each state's adult population", "state weights", "adjusted state weights", "95% CI" and "pooled". |
| Communicates the data | 2 / 5 | The map's colors spread a half-point range whose differences are mostly within each state's margin of error. Median maps turn 7-vs-8 into a two-color map. |
| Free of obvious bugs | 3 / 5 | Clipped pooled labels, the resize bug, a missing space, a no-op control. |
| Data site, not corporate dashboard | 4.5 / 5 | Yes: intervals in the chart and tooltips, pooling disclosed, CSV/PNG, data table. |

**Do these five first:**
1. Make the map honest about uncertainty (H1).
2. Offer Median only where it means something (H2).
3. Remove the "Adjusted state weights" toggle (H3).
4. Show one US reference number, plainly explained (H4).
5. Fix the phone layout and the clipped pooled labels (M2, M3).

---

## High

### H1 · The map overstates differences between states — Design
- **Seen:** for SFI, 2023, the map spreads 6.99 to 7.50 across seven shades. A typical state's 95% interval is about ±0.2 (Wyoming group: 7.21–7.66; Mississippi group: 6.88–7.38).
- In Chart view, almost every interval crosses the US line. The map, which has no uncertainty cue, shows strong regional patterns: the dark northern plains and Louisiana, the pale Nevada and Kansas.
- The ranking also reshuffles between waves (Louisiana tops 2023, New Mexico tops 2024), which is consistent with noise.
- **Fix (recommended):** color by difference from the US figure. Use a diverging ramp centered on the US value. Any state whose 95% interval includes the US value gets the neutral middle color.
  - Legend: "Lower than the US · About the same · Higher than the US"; shade depth shows the size of the gap.
  - The tooltip keeps the exact value and interval.
  - This is an encoding choice, not a text takeaway.
- **Alternative:** keep the value ramp, but overlay a light hatch on states that aren't distinguishable from the US.

### H2 · Median on 0–10 answers makes a dramatic two-color map — Design
- **Seen:** Happiness, Median. Every state's median is 7 or 8, so the map is split into two colors, beige and dark teal, like an election map. The legend still shows a seven-step 7.00–8.00 ramp.
- A shift of a few percent of people across the 7/8 boundary flips a state.
- The US overall line shows "7.00 n = 38,120" with no interval.
- **Fix:** offer Median only for measures with many distinct values: composite scores, age, counts. For 0–10 and Likert items, disable it with a reason ("Median isn't useful for 0–10 answers; most states share the same whole number"), or hide it.

### H3 · "Adjusted state weights" changes nothing — Bug / UX
- **Seen:** for 2024 and Midyear the checkbox is enabled. Ticking it changes the subtitle to "state weights, adjusted", but every number stays the same.
- **Checked:** the API returns identical estimates and intervals (max difference 2 × 10⁻¹⁵) for SFI, LONELY and ATTEND_SVCS.
- **Cause:** in the release, `w_state_adj_c2 = w_state_c2 × one constant per state`. The ratio's min equals its max in every state group. A per-state rescaling can't move a within-state mean or its interval.
- For 2023, the box is disabled with "The release has no adjusted state weight for Wave 1, 2023", which is more jargon for a control that has no effect.
- **Fix:** remove the control, its URL param (`adj`) and the reason line. Note the finding in the ADR. If the adjusted weight matters anywhere, it's only for a pooled national figure, which this page doesn't show.

### H4 · Two different "US overall" numbers — Design / data question
- **Seen:** the top line says "US overall (state weights): **7.22** [7.20, 7.25]". The footnote says "On the national weight, the US overall figure is 7.11 [7.07, 7.16]". The Atlas page shows 7.11 for the US.
- The two intervals don't overlap, and a first-time reader can't tell which number is "the US".
- **Fix:**
  - Keep the state-weight figure as the comparison line; it's the like-for-like comparator. Label it "US, from these state figures: 7.22".
  - Add one plain sentence: "Other pages use the national weights, which give 7.11; the two weightings adjust to different population totals."
  - Ask the study team whether a 0.11 gap between the two calibrations is expected.

## Medium

### M1 · Pooled states read as each state's own value — Design
- Pooling links non-neighbors (Delaware + Mississippi + West Virginia; Alaska + Hawaii + Montana), and all members wear the group's color.
- The large-area group (North Dakota + South Dakota + Wyoming) is often at an extreme — top shade for SFI and for "Has someone to confide in" — and dominates the map by area.
- The dashed outline is subtle, and hovering one member doesn't highlight the others.
- **Fix:**
  - Add a light hatch to pooled states on top of the dashed outline.
  - On hover or focus, outline every member of the group.
  - List the groups under the legend ("Pooled: ND·SD·WY, AK·HI·MT, ME·NH·RI·VT, DE·MS·WV").

### M2 · Chart view: pooled labels are clipped — Bug
- The labels render as "k Montana (pooled)", "k Vermont (pooled)" and "st Virginia (pooled)". "North Dakota, South Dakota & Wyoming (pooled)" shows only "Wyoming (pooled)", which misstates the group.
- **Cause:** the same fixed label gutter in `RankedBar` as Correlates M3; the Correlates prompt's fix (fit the gutter, wrap to two lines) covers it.
- For pooled rows, use the short form "ND · SD · WY (pooled)" with the full names in the tooltip.

### M3 · Phone: tiny map, hidden chart, "hover" copy — Design
- **Seen at 390 px:**
  - The map is 358 × 224. Rhode Island, Delaware, DC and the New England states are too small to tap.
  - The Chart view, the better phone view with intervals, is hidden in "More options — view, sort, weights", along with search and Topic.
  - The footnote says "Hover a state".
- **Fix:**
  - Show the Map/Chart switch at every width; consider Chart as the phone default.
  - Keep search visible.
  - Let a tap select a state and show its value, interval and n in a readout under the map.
  - Copy: "Tap or hover a state".

### M4 · The map doesn't widen on resize — Bug (site-wide)
Going 390 → 1280 px left the map at 358 px until a reload. This is the same `usePlot` bug as Correlates M5 and is covered by the Correlates prompt's task 6. Verify it here after that lands.

### M5 · Jargon — Design
- **Lede:** "…on weights calibrated to each state's adult population, beside the country's overall figure." Replace with: "How each US state compares with the country as a whole. Small states are grouped so every figure rests on enough people."
- **Subtitle:** drop "state weights"; the lede and "How these numbers are made" cover it.
- **Tooltip:** "7.44 · North Dakota, South Dakota & Wyoming (grouped) · likely between 7.21 and 7.66".

### M6 · Dark mode: the lowest states sink into the page — Design
In dark mode, the lowest shade (Nevada, Kansas, Ohio, New York) is nearly the page color, and borders are dark, so those states read as holes. The "no estimate" swatch is close to the low shades too. Fix: start the dark ramp lighter or use a lighter state stroke in dark mode, and give "no estimate" a hatch rather than a grey.

### M7 · No hover highlight on the map — Design
Hovering shows a tooltip but doesn't outline the state. Outline it (and its group, per M1). The data table remains the keyboard path; that's fine.

## Low

- **L1** Missing space in the footnote: "n = 38,299.How these numbers are made".
- **L2** The view label "Chart" is vague, and the URL says `view=bars` while the chart is dots. Rename it "Ranked list" (`view=ranked`, keeping `bars` as an alias).
- **L3** DC appears only as the "no estimate" swatch, too small to see. Say "District of Columbia: too few respondents" in the footnote.
- **L4** The controls reflow when the wave changes: the adjusted checkbox moves between its own row, with the reason, and inline. Moot once H3 removes it.
- **L5** Download CSV · PNG drops below the subtitle when the subtitle wraps (Midyear). This is shared `ChartFigure` behavior; keep the links top-right.
- **L6** For yes/no measures, "Answer level: Yes | No" only flips to 100 − x. Consider hiding it for binary measures.
- **L7** The chart's US reference line is labelled only at the bottom, about 1,100 px below the first row. Label it at the top too.

## What works

- Map and Chart are already a one-chart-at-a-time switch, the pattern you want elsewhere.
- Pooling is disclosed on the legend, the outline and the tooltip. Intervals are in the tooltip and drawn in Chart view.
- Chart view has a US reference line, Sort/Order controls that appear only when relevant, CSV/PNG and a data table.
- All state is in the URL; no console errors; Alaska and Hawaii insets are clean.
- The midyear measures work, with sensible wave switching.

## Things you may not notice because you know the site

1. Most of the map's color differences are within each state's margin of error.
2. The adjusted-weights box never changes a number.
3. "US overall" is 7.22 at the top of this page, 7.11 in its footnote, and 7.11 on Atlas.
4. Median on a 0–10 question paints the country in two colors.
5. "Wyoming (pooled)" is really three states.
6. On a phone, the view with intervals is hidden, and the copy says "hover".
