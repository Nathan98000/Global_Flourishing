## Summary

1. **Round 2's fixes mostly hold.** Of G1–G18:
   - 13 are fixed;
   - 4 are partly fixed (G4 US States' header, G5 Find a state, G6 Segments, G18's search);
   - 1 regressed elsewhere (G10: Distribution is fixed, but its axis title now overlaps a panel name on a phone).

   Highlights:
   - thin estimates are hollow and starred, with "Few people — treat with care" in the card;
   - Segments ranks Indonesia first;
   - downloads explain themselves;
   - the Atlas's first dot on a phone is at 594 px (round 2: 757).
2. **On every Distribution chart a screen reader is told "No estimates to show" (H1, Blocker).** Four panels are drawn, but the code passes an empty list to the spoken summary.
3. **The keyboard reads 6 ranked charts row by row, and 6 other charts not at all (H2).** Those six are What Matters' two grids, Change, Segments, Compare two's grid, Distribution and the map. There, a keyboard user can't open any card with its 95% CI and n. Segments is the worst: none of its numbers is visible without a card. Methods says What Matters can be read by keyboard; only its "Other questions" view can.
4. **A screen reader hears nothing when the year, the statistic or the order changes (H3).** The "Updated: …" line stays word for word the same. And reading through a chart, it hears country names without their values.
5. **Phones:**
   - **Find a state** does nothing with reduced motion on (H4).
   - **About ten kinds of control** are under the 44 px touch rule; in Safari every drop-down is 21 px tall (H5).
   - **Correlates' answer** sits below the first screen, at 933 px (H8).
   - **Change's key** overlaps its sort label (H15).
6. **When the live service is down, the notice shoves the page down** (layout shift 0.26–0.63; under 0.1 counts as good) and repeats the page's own message (H6). With no retry, one failed check marks the service offline for the whole visit. That is the likely cause of the deployed Atlas's 0.73 Lighthouse run.
7. **On a slow phone (H7):**
   - the page is blank for 2.8 s;
   - three data files are downloaded twice, because their preloads are thrown away;
   - the API sends 236–594 kB of uncompressed JSON per Change view.

   The deployed Atlas scores 0.73–0.87 for performance on Lighthouse's mobile preset; ADR-0010 recorded 0.98.
8. **Everyday friction:**
   - the question search ranks "SFI: …" composites above the plain match ("satisfied" puts Life satisfaction 3rd; "meaning" puts SFI: meaning & purpose 6th) (H9);
   - every page forgets the question and year you chose on the last (H10): the keyboard task took 93 keypresses, 24 of them re-choosing what was already chosen;
   - Segments with two splits is still about 22 phone screens long (H11).
9. **What works:**
   - the keyboard walk itself: focus is always visible; the picker, the Options sheet and Download ▾ open, trap and close correctly;
   - colour is never the only carrier of meaning;
   - reduced motion is respected;
   - 200% zoom reflows cleanly;
   - Lighthouse accessibility is 1.00 on all 36 runs;
   - axe finds nothing on any page's first view, except What Matters' "Within a country" at 390 (H16);
   - WebKit matches Chromium.
10. **If you do three things:**
    - fix the Distribution summary and make the "Updated" line and row names carry the view's state and values (H1 + H3, both small);
    - make Find a state work with reduced motion and lift every phone control to 44 px (H4 + H5, small);
    - give Change and Segments the keyboard rows the ranked charts already have (H2, medium).

## Findings

**Severity:**
- **Blocker:** misleads, or is unusable at some width or by some means.
- **Major:** a visitor is slowed or confused.
- **Minor:** a careful eye notices.

**Kind:**
- **Usability:** a visitor is slowed, confused or misled.
- **Accessibility:** a visitor using a keyboard, a screen reader, zoom or a colour-vision deficiency is blocked or slowed.
- **Taste:** it would look better.

Screenshot names are relative to `shots/` unless a folder is given. A proposal that crosses an owner decision or a project rule says so in bold.

### H1 · Every Distribution chart tells a screen reader "No estimates to show" — Blocker · accessibility

- **Page:** Atlas, `/?outcome=LIFE_SAT&stat=distribution` (every 0–10 question's Distribution), at every width, in every browser.
- **Evidence:**
  - `aria/atlas-dist-1280.yml`, line 55: `img "Life satisfaction (2023 · the four with the highest mean) by country. No estimates to show."`
  - **The cause:** `AtlasView.tsx:515` passes `stat === 'distribution' ? [] : displayRows` to `summarizeExtremes`, so the summary always has no rows.
- **What's wrong:** the chart is an image to assistive technology, so this label is all a screen-reader user gets. It says the opposite of what is drawn.
- **Why it matters:** a blind visitor is told there is no data and leaves.
- **Change:** a summary for the histogram from served numbers. Name the four countries in their order with each one's mean (the by-country file the panel order already reads), and the most common answer per country, or the share at the top answer. Example: "Life satisfaction, 2023: how people in Indonesia, Mexico, Egypt and Poland answered from 0 to 10 — means 7.99, 7.85, 7.69 and 7.52." Picking highest and lowest of served values is what `summarizeExtremes` already does, so no new statistic is computed. Add a test that the label never says "No estimates" when rows are drawn.
- **Effort:** small.

### H6 · When the live service is down, its notice shoves the page down and repeats itself — Major · usability

- **Pages:** every data page with :8098 unreachable (`logs/brk.log`, `off-*.png`). The deployed Atlas, Lighthouse run 2 (`lh/deployed-atlas-mobile-2.json`).
- **What's wrong:**
  - **The shift:** the notice is inserted above the page after the first paint. Layout shift on the Atlas is 0.26, on Median 0.29, on What Matters 0.31, and on Segments and the Codebook **0.63** (0.1 is "good"). On the deployed site, Lighthouse's second run caught `main` moving by 0.30.

  - **On pages that need the service,** the page's own message only.
  - **Retry the health check once** after about 3 s before declaring the service offline.
  - **Owner question 3.** Mockup: `docs/reviews/design-3-2026-10-22-mockups.html`, panel B.
- **Effort:** small.

### H7 · On a slow phone the page is blank for nearly 3 seconds and some files come down twice — Major · usability

- **Pages:** all, on Slow 4G with a 4× CPU slowdown at 390 (`films/strip-*.png`, `logs/film.log`), locally and on `flourish-atlas.pages.dev`.
- **What's wrong:**

    | Deployed, run 2 | 0.73 | 3.3 s | 0.306 (H6) |
    | This build, locally | 0.94 | 2.7 s | 0.065 |
    | ADR-0010's record | 0.98 | — | — |
