The controls at the top of each page — three ways to reorganise them

Mockups for the Flourish Atlas design review of 21 October 2026, drawn with the app's own colours, type and control styles. Labels are illustrative. The charts are grey stand-ins: no number on this page comes from the data.

How to use it: pick a layout below. Each one shows the Atlas, Correlates and Segments as a 1280 px screen would draw them (the app's page column is 896 px wide there) and the Atlas on a 390 px phone. Controls marked ▾ open where it helps to see the flow: the question picker, the phone's options sheet, and sorting from the chart's own column labels. The full reasoning is in design-2026-10-21.md, section “Controls”.

Today, for comparison

The Atlas as it stands on claude/charts-only-methods, simplified. At 1280 × 800 the first country appears about four-fifths of the way down the first screen; only six of the 23 rows are visible before scrolling.

A · Ask it as a sentence

Correlates already asks “How do answers to … relate to …?”. Option A gives every page a sentence like that. Each noun is a button: the question opens the question picker, the year and the countries open small lists. How the chart is drawn (average or middle value, order) sits as two quiet words in the chart header.

  • Other pages: Change — “How did life satisfaction change from 2023 to 2024 for the same people in all countries?” What Matters — “What matters most to people in Brazil, by age?” US States — “How do people rate life satisfaction in each state, 2023?”
  • Fixes: the controls read as the visitor's own question; the sentence is the summary; three pickers become one.
  • Costs: long question names (“Feelings about household income”) break the sentence onto three lines at 1280 and five on a phone; every page needs its own grammar, and answers like “More than once a week” do not fit a sentence slot; settings that are not nouns (sort, the correlation type, adjusted weights) still need somewhere else to live. Large.

B · A question bar on top, a toolbar on the chart Recommended

The top of every page holds only what you are looking at and when and where: one question button (search and browse by topic in one panel — the Correlates picker), then Year, then Countries, always in that order. Everything about how it is drawn moves into the chart's own header, beside the downloads; sorting moves onto the chart's column labels, as the What Matters matrix already works.

  • Other pages: Change — Question · Countries (its years are fixed: 2023 → 2024). What Matters — its three views stay as tabs; Within a country gets Country · Split by on top. US States — Question · Year; Map/Chart, Show and the adjusted-weights switch go in the chart toolbar, plus “Find a state”.
  • Fixes: Topic, Measure and Or search become one control; Sort and Order become one click on a label; the same three things sit in the same place on every page; at 1280 the controls take one row (about 70 px, down from about 190 px); on a phone, two controls and one Options button.
  • Costs: the picker joins the first page load (about 5 kB gzipped, est.; 206 → about 211 of 250); sort-by-label needs a visible arrow and keyboard focus; revises the two-step picker decision (browse-by-topic survives inside the panel). Medium.

C · A settings panel beside the chart

On a desktop every control moves into a column at the left, grouped under three headings — Question, Year & place, Display — and the chart takes the rest. On a tablet or a phone the column becomes a summary line over the chart with an Edit button that opens the same form as a sheet.

  • Other pages: the same three groups everywhere; Correlates' tabs stay above the panel; What Matters' views stay as tabs.
  • Fixes: the chart is the biggest thing on the first screen; one form, one order; the summary line answers “what am I looking at?”.
  • Costs: either the chart loses about 290 px of width at 1280 (896 → about 600), or the page grows past its 896 px reading column as drawn here (1184 px) and stops lining up with Methods; the look turns from publication to dashboard (ADR-0012); on a phone every change is two taps (Edit, then the control). Large.

Owner's changes, 22 Oct 2026: Mean, Median, Distribution and “95% CI” keep their names (not “Average”, “Middle value”, “All answers” or “likely range”), and nothing sorts A–Z — the name column is not a sort button; only the value label sorts, and a second click reverses it. Words used in the mockups

Wave → Year · Statistic → Show · Mean → Average · Median → Middle value · Distribution → All answers · Sort + Order → Order (or a click on a column label) · Topic + Measure + Or search → Question · Break down by / Second breakdown → Split by … then by … · Correlation type → Measured as · Country + scope → Where. The URL parameters (wave, stat, sort, dir, countries, by, method, country, view) do not change.
